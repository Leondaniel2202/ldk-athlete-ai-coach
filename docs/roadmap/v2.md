## Goal

Introduce a minimal custom frontend for training planning experimentation and validation, strengthen backend ownership of core logic, and expand the AI layer from basic analysis into a meaningful personalized coaching and content-generation capability, while keeping Notion as the primary day-to-day operational system during V2.

The goal of V2 is to build the next major layer of the platform without disrupting current personal usage or forcing premature migration away from Notion.

---

## Why V2 Exists

V1 established a strong backend foundation, but the system is still heavily dependent on Notion for planning, tracking, and parts of the business logic.

V2 exists to:

- begin building a custom frontend early, allowing practical experience with UI development and validation of planning workflows
- move critical business logic from Notion into the backend to improve consistency, control, and testability
- make the backend more production-like and usable as a real system, not just a mirrored data layer
- make AI a first-class product capability, not just an analysis add-on
- create real room to learn and apply AI engineering through structured generation, context design, and personalized coaching workflows
- ensure the system evolves in a way that remains fully usable for day-to-day training, without requiring immediate migration away from Notion

The frontend introduced in V2 is not intended to replace Notion yet, but to prepare for a future transition by validating design, workflows, and architecture. During V2, some business logic will temporarily exist in both the backend and Notion to preserve day-to-day operational usability while transitioning logic ownership toward the backend.

---

## Scope

V2 focuses on introducing a minimal frontend for planning experimentation, strengthening backend ownership, and significantly expanding the AI layer, while keeping Notion as the operational system.

The scope includes the following areas:

---

### Frontend (Planning – Experimental)

- Minimal frontend for creating and managing:
  - plans
  - phases
  - workouts (week-based)
- Basic views for organizing and inspecting training structure
- Use of a separate development database for testing and experimentation
- No requirement to support full operational workflows

---

### Backend Logic Ownership

- Implementation of core business logic in the backend, including:
  - workout, phase, and plan status logic
  - basic training load calculations
  - week-based grouping and structure logic
- Temporary duplication of some logic in Notion to maintain operational usability
- Backend treated as the canonical implementation

---

### API Maturity

- Introduction of write-capable endpoints for:
  - plans
  - phases
  - workouts
- Clear and consistent API contracts for frontend consumption
- Improved validation and data consistency
- API support for AI-driven creation workflows and structured AI outputs

---

### AI Capability Expansion

- Improvements to existing analysis capabilities
- Expansion, refinement, and standardization of structured AI outputs exposed through the API
- Refactoring and unification of AI services
- Introduction of structured athlete context for AI use, such as:
  - goals
  - current event focus
  - preferences
  - constraints
  - training background
- Introduction of coaching persona support to allow different coaching styles or lenses
- Support for AI-assisted generation of:
  - workouts
  - phases
  - initial plan structures or scaffolds
- Exploration of light historical grounding for analysis and generation using existing system context
- Preparation for future:
  - Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
  - agentic AI workflows

---

### Data Model Improvements

- Refinement of database schema
- Introduction of stricter validation rules where feasible
- Alignment of backend model with long-term system goals
- Support for storing and exposing richer AI-related context structures where needed

---

## Out of Scope

The following areas are explicitly not part of V2:

---

### Full Replacement of Notion

- The frontend will not replace Notion for day-to-day usage
- No requirement to migrate all workflows away from Notion

---

### Write-Back to Notion

- No bidirectional synchronization between backend and Notion
- Backend will not write data back to Notion

---

### Advanced Frontend Features

- No full dashboards or analytics UI
- No advanced visualization (graphs, trends)
- No deeply AI-native frontend experience

---

### Advanced AI Capabilities Beyond V2 Scope

- No full production-grade RAG platform
- No agentic AI systems acting autonomously within the system
- No fully automated end-to-end coaching loop
- No broad autonomous plan adaptation without explicit user involvement

---

### Direct External Integrations

- No direct ingestion from Apple Health or other systems into the backend
- Existing Notion-based ingestion flow remains unchanged

---

## Workstreams

V2 is organized into several workstreams that can be developed in parallel and iteratively refined.

### 1. Frontend (Planning UI)

Development of a minimal frontend focused on planning workflows, enabling creation and organization of plans, phases, and workouts.

---

### 2. Backend Logic Ownership

Migration of core business logic from Notion into the backend, establishing the backend as the canonical implementation.

---

### 3. API Maturity

Enhancement of the API to support frontend interaction, including write operations, validation, and clear contracts.

---

### 4. AI Capability Expansion

Expansion of the AI layer from analysis-only toward personalized and structured coaching support, including athlete context, persona handling, and generation of workouts, phases, and initial plan structures.

---

### 5. Data Model Improvements

Refinement of the database schema and validation rules to align with long-term system goals.

## Success Criteria

V2 is considered successful when the following conditions are met:

---

### Frontend

- A minimal frontend exists that allows:
  - creation of plans, phases, and workouts
  - basic organization of training structure
- The frontend is usable for experimentation and validation of planning workflows
- The frontend is built with a clean and extensible architecture

---

### Backend

- Core business logic (status, basic metrics, week structure) is implemented in the backend
- The backend acts as the canonical implementation of this logic
- Data validation and consistency are improved compared to V1

---

### API

- The API supports both read and write operations for core entities
- Endpoints are stable, consistent, and usable by the frontend
- API contracts are clearly defined
- AI-related endpoints and schemas are structured enough to support generation workflows

---

### AI

- Existing analysis capabilities are improved and more reliable
- Structured AI outputs are consistent and usable for further processing
- Athlete context is integrated as a meaningful AI input
- Persona-based coaching behavior is supported in a clear and reusable way
- The system can generate structured workout and phase outputs
- The system can support at least initial plan scaffolding through AI
- The AI layer is organized in a way that supports future expansion (RAG, agents)

---

### System Usability

- The system remains fully usable for day-to-day training through Notion
- Backend, frontend, and AI development do not disrupt existing workflows
- The system feels like a cohesive platform rather than separate components

---

### Overall

- The system clearly progresses toward independence from Notion
- AI becomes a real product capability alongside frontend and backend evolution
- The foundation for future AI capabilities and frontend expansion is established
- The system is stable enough to continue building on top of it in V3
